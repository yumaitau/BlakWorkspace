# Notes and Knowledge

Enable Notes for pilot spaces. Templates must avoid cultural claims. Backup Notes data stores per upstream guidance.

Blak Knowledge uses **Docmost** (BW-055), not XWiki. Do not enable XWiki in Blak default profiles. Do not enable XWiki and Docmost together. Until Docmost is pinned and licensed under BW-055, keep Knowledge disabled rather than turning XWiki back on by default.
