# [BW-005] Define Indigenous governance review process

## Problem / outcome
Brand and cultural content need a review process so the project does not invent endorsements or cultural assets.

## Context / upstream evidence
Label needs:cultural-review. No fabricated community endorsements, cultural assets, or sovereignty claims. Process under docs/governance/. Applies to brand packs and cultural materials in Drive or Docmost Knowledge (BW-055).

This issue is part of epic BW-E01 under milestone M0. Australian English. blocked:decision until owners accept the process.

## In scope
- Written governance boundary
- When cultural review required
- Who can approve cultural copy
- Escalation for disputed claims

## Non-goals

- Claiming community endorsement
- IRAP/PROTECTED assertions
