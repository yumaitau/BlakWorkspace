# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Blak Workspace a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting differing viewpoints
- Accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community and customers
- Showing care for Indigenous cultural protocols and review requirements

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:
- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information
- Inventing cultural endorsements or misusing cultural materials
- Other conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and may ban temporarily or permanently any contributor for other behaviours they deem inappropriate.

## Reporting

Report incidents to the repository maintainers via a private channel. Maintainers will review and respond as they are able.
